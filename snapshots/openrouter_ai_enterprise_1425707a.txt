Requests to eu.openrouter.ai that run on shared capacity go to shared provider endpoints in Europe (the EU/EEA, the UK, and Switzerland), and those to us.openrouter.ai go to shared provider endpoints in the US. Requests served by your own BYOK key go to your deployment's region, which you control. A private endpoint is eligible when its provider region, or the region you declared for it, is in that region. A declared region is your attestation, so make sure it matches where the endpoint runs.
