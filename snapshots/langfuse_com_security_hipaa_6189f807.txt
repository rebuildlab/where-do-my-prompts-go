HIPAA Compliance & Langfuse Business Associate Agreement (BAA)
Langfuse offers a dedicated HIPAA-ready cloud region for eligible customers with healthcare or life-sciences workloads. This region is separate from the EU, US, and Japan Langfuse Cloud regions.
Eligible customers can enter into a signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with Langfuse for HIPAA-regulated use. Customers with HIPAA requirements should
complete the BAA via Ironclad
before processing Protected Health Information (PHI) in Langfuse.
Availability
Data region:
https://hipaa.cloud.langfuse.com
Location:
Oregon, AWS
us-west-2
Eligible plans:
Pro plan or higher
Signed BAA
Customers on Pro plan or higher in the HIPAA data region can
complete and sign the BAA via Ironclad
. Do not process PHI in Langfuse until the signed BAA is in place.
How to get started
Create a fresh Langfuse Cloud account in the
HIPAA data region
.
Subscribe to
Pro plan or higher
.
Complete and sign the BAA via Ironclad
before processing PHI.
Configure your application to send HIPAA-regulated data only to
https://hipaa.cloud.langfuse.com
.
The HIPAA region is completely separate from the
other data regions
. Data migration is possible via the
data migration cookbook
.
Self-hosted deployments
The HIPAA data region and the BAA cover Langfuse Cloud, where Langfuse operates the infrastructure. In a
self-hosted
deployment Langfuse does not receive or process your data, so no BAA with Langfuse is involved and the HIPAA safeguards are yours to implement in your environment. Relevant building blocks:
encryption
,
data masking
before or at ingestion,
data retention
,
audit logs
,
SSO
, and
network isolation
.
