What options are available for high compliance concerns?
Helicone offers several solutions for organizations with strict compliance needs:
Comprehensive Security Measures:
Explore our
Data and Security
section for details on SOC 2 compliance and other security protocols.
Data Residency Choices:
Ensure GDPR compliance by selecting between US and EU data regions.
Request and Response Privacy:
Use our
Omit Logs feature
to prevent storing request and response bodies in long-term storage.
Asynchronous Logging:
Implement our
async logging solution
to capture metadata (tokens, latency, time to first token) without storing actual content.
Self-Hosting Solutions:
Consider our
self-hosting options
to maintain complete control over your data environment.
