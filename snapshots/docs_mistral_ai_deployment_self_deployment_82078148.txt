Self-Deployment
Mistral AI models can be
self-deployed on your own infrastructure
through various
inference engines. We recommend using
vLLM
, a
highly-optimized Python-only serving framework which can expose an OpenAI-compatible
API.
Other inference engine alternatives include
TensorRT-LLM
and
TGI
.
You can also leverage specific tools to facilitate infrastructure management, such as
SkyPilot
or
Cerebrium
.
Tip
For full-stack enterprise deployment, from efficient model inference to team management, we recommend
reaching out to us
.
